In Shotgun Sunday, you are a human fighting against zombies that could hold weapons! You need to scavenge for weapons and medkits and try to survive as long as possible.
Each of the zombies drops a coin when defeated and depending on the difficulty, you gain more coins! (Difficulty also manages the amount of zombies, amount of weapons, amount of healthpacks, etc.)
